# Pushpin or Poetry

An interactive study guide and pass-and-play quiz game on **Frédéric Bastiat** and **John Stuart Mill**, plus E. K. Hunt's critique. Built for ECO 4343 (History of Economic Thought).

**Play it:** https://hyeung024.github.io/pushpin-or-poetry/

## What's inside

- **Study:** Bastiat's theory and Hunt's critique, Mill's theory and Hunt's critique, a side-by-side table, and the French socialists Bastiat was answering. Includes a lab that tests Bastiat's distribution "law" (a lower profit rate is not a lower profit share).
- **Flashcards:** 22 key terms to flip, filter by topic, and shuffle.
- **Play:** 2–4 players on one screen, 64 questions in 5 decks (Bastiat, Mill, Hunt's Critique, Who Said It?, Context). Each turn you pick a deck, then bet **Pushpin** (safe: +100) or **Poetry** (risky: +250 / −100), after Bentham's "pushpin is as good as poetry" and Mill's reply. After each round, a Mill-style **commercial crisis** may wipe out 20% of the leader's points. At the end you get a list of every missed question to review.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app in one file. Open it in any browser; no install needed.
- `Bastiat-Mill-Summary.md`: written study summary of the chapter.

Content is based on Hunt & Lautzenheiser, *History of Economic Thought: A Critical Perspective* (2011), ch. 8 (pp. 174–201). The book itself is not included.
